# Business Profile - ARB Corporation Limited (ASX: ARB)

Grounded in the FY2025 Appendix 4E. Figures trace to `data/SOURCES.md`.

## Entity

Largest Australian aftermarket 4WD accessories business. Vertically integrated
design, manufacture, sale and distribution. Operations in every Australian
state and in the US, Thailand, New Zealand, UAE, UK and Czech Republic.
30 June year, AUD, fully franked.

## Channels

| Channel | FY2025 sales | FY2024 sales | Change |
|---|--:|--:|--:|
| Australian Aftermarket | $403.3m | $404.1m | (0.2%) |
| Exports | $267.0m | $229.4m | 16.4% |
| Original Equipment | $59.7m | $59.6m | 0.1% |

No channel EBITDA is disclosed.

## FY2025 print

Sales $729.9m (+5.3%). EBIT $136.1m. NPAT $97.5m. Cash $69.2m, no debt.
Materials $315.7m. Inventories $249.1m after H2 destock. Franking credits
$112.0m. Special dividend 50c plus final 35c, both fully franked, declared
after balance date.

Profit fell despite sales growth: weak AUD/THB, US auto/steel/aluminium
tariffs, associate losses, and investment in people, Texas DC, and advertising.

## What the engine does not swallow

ORW/4WP associate, MITS Alloy / Christchurch acquisitions, lease accounting,
